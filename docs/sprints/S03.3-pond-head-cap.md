# Sprint 3.3 — pond follows head, not a unique downhill chute

**Status:** ready for oracle
**Depends on:** S03.2 green on main
**Out of scope:** plants, ET, Manning n tables, soil contour split, editing docs/.

## Why

S02.1 sent the whole pond to the single lowest-H neighbor. On a 6 m drop that empties the hill in one tick. After the valley fills, H_valley ≈ H_high and unique-min routing sloshes forever around a drowned lake.

Path of least resistance for **surface** water is lower **head** H = z + h, not lower z. A full valley at h ≈ 6.1 has H ≈ 8.1 and is a lake, not a drain.

Same-z pond spread is real when ΔH that way is nonzero, but it is tiny compared with an empty 6 m face. We do not invent an 80/20 split. We cap each edge so a steep face cannot swallow the whole bucket in one tick; leftover pond can then meet a same-z neighbor that has lower H.

**Soil** leftover m stays S03.2 (downslope z first). Contour seep in dirt is second-order next to Δz = 6 vs m ~ 0.05.

## Pond pass (replaces unique-min-H S02.1)

Locked constant: `R_max = 0.15` metres of water per edge per tick.

Snapshot H_i = z_i + h_i and h_i.

For each cell i with h_i > 0, collect every 4-neighbor j with H_j < H_i.

Let Δ_j = H_i − H_j > 0. Weight w_j = Δ_j / ΣΔ.

Desired send to j:

V_j = min( h_i * w_j,  0.5 * Δ_j,  R_max )

If Σ V_j > h_i, scale all V_j by h_i / Σ V_j.

Apply all sends simultaneously. No unique-min-H. Equal H ⇒ Δ = 0 ⇒ no flux that edge.
