# Sprint 16.2 — tree canopy bucket

**Status:** locked for oracle
**Depends on:** S15 band crowd, S16 seed rain.
**Does not change:** seed pulse. S16 still attempts once per tick. S16.1 is not locked.

## Problem

H_BAND = 0.05 m splits a tree crown. Two pines one tick apart differ by about 6 cm and never share a band, so A_b stays <= 1 and thin never fires.

## Rule

Herbs stay on 5 cm bands.
form=tree and form=shrub on a tile share one canopy bucket:
A_canopy = sum alpha * height_frac^2 over living tree/shrub occupants on that tile.
If A_canopy > 1, s = 1/A_canopy on alpha_eff and uptake. At T_CROWD=7 drop the weakest (lowest f_L * f_w * f_T * height_frac) in that bucket.
Herbs are not in the sum and are not removed by it.
A one-tick age gap must still compete. No RNG. No English-name branch. Catch_up uses the same bucket.
