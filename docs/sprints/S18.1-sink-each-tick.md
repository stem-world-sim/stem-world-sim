# Sprint 18.1 — time, not ticks (draft)

Not locked. Oracle does not start this until the user confirms.

A step only advances the clock by `tick_dt` days. No rule reads the step count.

## Clock and sinks

Delete `N_ET`. Hydro, evaporation, and uptake run every step, each scaled by `tick_dt`. `catch_up(K)` is K of the same step. Clock advances by K.

Rates already in days stay: infiltrate, drain, pond flow, evaporation, uptake, growth.

## Rain

`rain_per_day_m`. A step adds `rain_per_day × tick_dt`. Control is mm/day. Zero is a drought.

## Water store

A plant is a water battery, not a dry-day counter.

- Capacity `W_cap` scales with size and leaf thickness.
- Each step: soil water taken is added; the day's use is removed.
- Use scales with size. Capacity scales faster, so a larger plant lasts longer on the same dry soil.
- Dies when the store is empty.
- Remaining store returns to the top soil layer on death. Use is the only sink.

No catalog column is a measured reserve. Capacity is derived: base from `drain_ok`, thickness from SLA (low SLA = thicker leaf = more store per area), size from `height_frac`.

## Energy store

Light is the charge. Stored food is the battery.

- Capacity scales with size.
- Charge scales with received light and size. Below `L_min`, charge is 0.
- Respiration scales with size and runs every step.
- Dies when the store is empty.
- A shade plant charges at lower light because its `L_opt` is lower. That is the existing shade class, not a new trait.

## Seed

One attempt per elapsed day, capped by the tile occupant limit. A long step drops one per whole day of that step.

## Not batteries

Waterlog and submerge are lack of oxygen, not an empty water store. They stay a days-under-water limit, longer for `drain_ok` that contains W.

Crowd is area. The weakest in an over-full bucket is thinned after a size-scaled wait, not a fixed step count.

## Page

Elapsed time only. Rain labeled mm/day. Each plant shows water store and energy store as a fraction of capacity, and the reason if either is falling. No tick count.

## Open

The capacity and use numbers are calibrated, not measured. Confirm the shape before lock.
