# Sprint 2.1 — head equalize (pool, no ping-pong)

**Status:** ready for oracle
**Depends on:** S02 on main
**Why:** S02 sent min(h, ΔH) to a single neighbor. Two equal-z cells swap the whole puddle every tick.

## Goal

Surface water still runs downhill. On flat connected cells it settles to a shared pond (equal H). Further ticks do not oscillate.

## Routing change (replaces S02 flux + tie rule)

Keep: 4-neighbor grid, infiltrate-all then one simultaneous runoff pass, surface only, H = z + h, closed boundary, send only to neighbors with strictly lower H.

Replace:

1. Let S be 4-neighbors with strictly lower H. If S empty, send nothing.
2. Let H* be min H in S. Let T be every neighbor in S whose H equals H*.
3. Total volume leaving i: V = min(h_i, 0.5 * (H_i - H*)).
4. Split V equally across |T|. Snapshot H and h, then apply deltas.

Soil theta does not jump sideways.
