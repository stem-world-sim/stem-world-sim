# Sprint 19.1 — conditions and stores

**Status:** locked for oracle
**Depends on:** S19 (`b82d167`).

A plant holds stores. A step computes conditions. Conditions gate the fluxes that fill and empty the stores. The plant dies when any required store is empty.

## Stores

Held on the plant. Extensible by id. No match arm per id.

| Id | What it is | Capacity | Returns on death |
|---|---|---|---|
| `water` | Tissue water | 30 days of use at full size | Soil water |
| `energy` | Sugar and starch | 7 days of respiration at full size | Not returned |
| `nitrogen` | Tissue nitrogen | 30 days of use at full size | Soil nitrogen pool |

Pinned rates:

- Water use per day at full size: the existing daily uptake (`P_MAX / N_ET` after the S19 scale). Capacity = 30 days of that use.
- Energy use per day at full size: 1.0 store unit. Capacity = 7.
- Nitrogen use per day: 0.02 low, 0.05 moderate, 0.10 high, from catalog `fertility`. Soil start = 50 per tile. Capacity = 30 days of that use.
- Capacity scales with `height_frac` faster than use, so a larger plant lasts longer.

Drop the `co2` store. Delete `NUT_CO2`, `USE_CO2`, `CAP_DAYS_CO2`. Carbon is fixed into `energy`. It is not held as CO2.

## Conditions

Computed each step. Not stored on the plant.

| Condition | Gates | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Light | Carbon fixation | Existing canopy `L`. Below `L_min`, fixation is 0. |
| CO2 access | Carbon fixation | 1 in air. 0 when submerged (`pond >= height`) unless `takes_dissolved` is 1, in which case fixation draws the tile dissolved-carbon pool (start 30). |
| Root O2 | Uptake of soil water and soil-bound nutrients | 1 when the root zone is not waterlogged. 0 when waterlogged (`pond >= H_POND` or top layer at porosity) unless `takes_dissolved` is 1. Not a store. |
| Temperature | Fixation and respiration | Existing envelope. Out of range: fixation 0. |
| Soil water | Water-store recharge | Existing hydro. |
| Soil nitrogen | Nitrogen-store recharge | Tile soil-bound pool. |

Waterlogging without full submergence does not block CO2 access. It does block root uptake for a plant that cannot take dissolved gases.

## The step

Every step, scaled by `tick_dt`:

\[
E \leftarrow E + \mathrm{fix}(L,\ \mathrm{CO_2\text{-}access},\ W,\ T,\ N) - \mathrm{respire}(\mathrm{size},\ T)
\]

\[
W \leftarrow W + \mathrm{uptake}(\mathrm{soil\ water},\ \mathrm{root\ O_2}) - \mathrm{transpire}(\mathrm{size})
\]

\[
N \leftarrow N + \mathrm{uptake}(\mathrm{soil\ } N,\ \mathrm{root\ O_2}) - \mathrm{use}(\mathrm{size},\ \mathrm{fertility})
\]

Fixation is 0 if light is below `L_min`, or CO2 access is 0, or the water store is empty, or temperature is outside the envelope. Respiration runs every step. Root O2 at 0 means no soil uptake that step. Die when any required store is empty.

Clock, rain per day, seed one per day, and `catch_up(K) = K` live steps stay as in S19.
