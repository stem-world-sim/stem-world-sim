# Seed is not a linear pulse

S16.1 (one attempt per T_F) is skipped. A constant pulse is the wrong shape.

Later, after climate and animals exist, seed rain is a product of three catalog/state factors, not a tick rate:

- potential viable seeds from maturity (height_frac, age, form)
- pollination success in [0, 1] (neighbors, season, pollinator presence)
- dispersal success for that taxon's class (wind intensity, bird/animal density, water)

A ripe tree can hold a crop and then dump it in one wind event. Do not ship a once-per-T_F stand-in as the long-term rule.
